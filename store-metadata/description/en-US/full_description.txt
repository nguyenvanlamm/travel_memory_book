Travel Memory Book — Your personal travel journal that turns every trip into a beautiful digital photo book.

Unlike social media apps that pressure you to share and perform, Travel Memory Book is designed for one person: you. No likes, no comments, no followers, no feed. Just your memories, preserved exactly as you want to remember them.

KEY FEATURES:
📖 Create Travel Books — Each trip becomes its own digital photo book with cover, pages, and memories
📸 Smart Photo Import — Add dozens of photos at once, automatic EXIF reading (date, GPS, camera)
🗓️ Automatic Timeline — Photos auto-sorted by date, grouped by day
📍 Location Awareness — GPS from photos auto-mapped to place names
✍️ Personal Journal — Write memories for whole trips, specific days, or individual photos
📖 Book Viewer — Flip through your trip like a real photo book with smooth page animations
🌙 Dark/Light Mode — Beautiful themes optimized for photo viewing day or night
🔒 100% Private — No cloud, no tracking, no ads. Your data stays on your device
📊 Personal Statistics — Track your travels: countries, cities, photos, trips

PERFECT FOR:
• Travelers who want to remember every detail of their adventures
• Families documenting vacations and milestones
• Anyone who wants a private, beautiful way to revisit memories
• People tired of social media's pressure to share and perform
